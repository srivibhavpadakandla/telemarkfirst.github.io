The cargo, obstacle, and hinged target are additional teaching assemblies made
from official LDraw brick, plate, beam, axle, and pin files. Their source models
and full part list are in scripts/assets/fll-field-*.ldr and
scripts/assets/fll-field-models-ATTRIBUTION.txt in the Telemark repository.

The drivebase, lever tool, mount points, and lesson placement are Telemark
teaching assemblies. They are not a certified or official FIRST LEGO League
robot. No BrickLink Studio-only custom mesh is included in these new assets.
