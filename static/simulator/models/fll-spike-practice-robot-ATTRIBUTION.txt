Changes: extracted the BrickLink Studio LDraw model, converted it to a Three.js glTF asset,
rotated it to a vertical-up coordinate system, centered it, scaled it to centimeters, and
optimized the glTF mesh for web display. The simulator's animated attachment indicator is
separate from this model and does not represent an articulated part of the source build.

The conversion uses geometry from the LDraw Parts Library. Individual LDraw parts are
licensed under CC BY 2.0 and/or CC BY 4.0 as marked in their source files. Credit to the
LDraw parts authors and LDraw.org: https://www.ldraw.org/docs-main/licenses/legal-info.html
The original LDraw model export is in scripts/assets/fll-practice-robot.ldr; supplemental
geometry from the Studio export is in scripts/assets/fll-practice-robot-parts.ldr.
