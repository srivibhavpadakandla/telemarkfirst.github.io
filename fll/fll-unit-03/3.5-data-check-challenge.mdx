# Lesson F3.5: Data Check Challenge

Convert a planned distance from centimeters to millimeters once, use that value for a short drive, and print both the plan and the encoder estimate afterward. Calculate their difference to inspect the command, then measure actual travel separately on the physical robot because an encoder reading is not a ruler measurement.

## Worked example

~~~python
target_cm = 30
target_mm = target_cm * 10
robot.reset()
robot.straight(target_mm)
error_mm = robot.distance() - target_mm
print("target mm:", target_mm)
print("encoder error mm:", error_mm)
~~~

## Simulator practice

Change target_cm twice. Confirm that both route and output change without editing the DriveBase call.

## On the robot

Compare encoder distance with a ruler measurement for three trials and explain any difference.

## Check and adjust

The printed target is 300 mm for 30 cm, and your log separates encoder error from physical error. If encoder error is near zero but the ruler shows a miss, inspect slip.
