# Lesson F9.5: Sensor Route Challenge

This challenge uses a sensor reading to end a drive, with a timeout for a missed line. The goal is to stop at the dark line, then test what happens when the threshold cannot be reached. The two outcomes should both leave the robot stopped before the next field object.

## Worked example

The first straight drive puts the sensor nearer the line. The continuous drive then ends on a dark reading or after eight seconds. The example prints the final reflection, and the elapsed time can also be printed to distinguish the two outcomes.

~~~python
robot.straight(200)
watch = StopWatch()
robot.drive(90, 0)
while color_sensor.reflection() > 35 and watch.time() < 8000:
    wait(10)
robot.stop()
print("Light:", color_sensor.reflection())
~~~

## Simulator practice

Use the sample route for a first line stop. Changing its threshold to a value below zero should force the timeout path; compare the final position and stop event after both runs.

## On the robot

Five physical starts from the same launch mark can show how much the stop distance varies. Record any run that misses the line as well as the successful ones.

## Check and adjust

Both runs should end with a stopped drive base. If the line is missed, the reading and elapsed time can help determine whether the threshold or the route needs adjustment.
