# Lesson F9.2: Stop at a Dark Line

`DriveBase.drive()` and `ColorSensor.reflection()` can stop a route at a dark line, provided the loop also has a timeout. Place the sensor ahead of the wheel contact point if the wheels must stop before the line, and measure a braking offset because higher speed can carry the robot farther after detection.

## Worked example

~~~python
watch = StopWatch()
robot.drive(100, 0)
while color_sensor.reflection() > 35 and watch.time() < 3000:
    wait(10)
robot.stop()
print("Stop mm:", robot.distance())
~~~

## Simulator practice

Run at 100 and 200 mm/s. Compare the final distance and line position.

## On the robot

Measure the gap from sensor to wheels and the overshoot at both speeds. Use the slower speed if the mission tolerance is narrow.

## Check and adjust

The program stops for a line and for a missing line within the time limit. If the robot overshoots, reduce speed or account for sensor offset.
