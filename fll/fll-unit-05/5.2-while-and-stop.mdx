# Lesson F5.2: Repeat Until a Cue

A `while` loop repeats while its condition remains `True`, so the sensor reading must be updated inside the loop. Start a continuous drive, check reflection, and stop the drive base when the condition ends. A short `wait(10)` spaces the readings. A later lesson adds a timeout for the case where no line appears.

## Worked example

~~~python
robot.drive(100, 0)
while color_sensor.reflection() > 35:
    wait(10)
robot.stop()
~~~

## Simulator practice

Drive toward the line and confirm the robot stops after the reading falls below 35 percent.

## On the robot

Place the robot near the line and run at low speed. Check that the sensor reaches the line before the wheels cross the target area.

## Check and adjust

You can point to the line that refreshes the reading and the line that stops motion. If the loop never ends, refresh the reading and add a timeout.

Reference: [Pybricks ColorSensor reference](https://docs.pybricks.com/en/stable/pupdevices/colorsensor.html).
