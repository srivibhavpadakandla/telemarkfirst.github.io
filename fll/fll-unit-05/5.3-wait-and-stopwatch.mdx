# Lesson F5.3: Wait and Measure Time

`wait()` pauses for milliseconds, while `StopWatch.time()` reports elapsed milliseconds since the watch started. Use a short wait to space sensor polls and measure a maneuver instead of guessing its duration from the number of commands. A blocking wait does not check sensors until it finishes.

## Worked example

~~~python
watch = StopWatch()
robot.straight(300)
elapsed_ms = watch.time()
print("Elapsed ms:", elapsed_ms)
wait(100)
~~~
