# Lesson F1.1: Color and Reflected Light

A ColorSensor reports reflected light as a percentage, and a dark line usually reads lower than the surrounding mat. Print readings over the actual light and dark surfaces with the sensor at its normal height, then choose a threshold between those measurements. Sensor height and surface finish can change the numbers, so a threshold from another robot is only a starting guess.

## Worked example

~~~python
print("Light:", color_sensor.reflection())
robot.drive(100, 0)
while color_sensor.reflection() > 35:
    wait(10)
robot.stop()
~~~

## Simulator practice

Run toward the dark line. Adjust 35 only after reading the simulated light and dark values.

## On the robot

Mount the sensor at a fixed height and log five readings on each surface. Record the midpoint threshold and three line-stop results.

## Check and adjust

Your threshold separates the measured surfaces and the program stops at the line. If light and dark readings overlap, fix sensor height before the threshold.

Reference: [Pybricks ColorSensor reference](https://docs.pybricks.com/en/stable/pupdevices/colorsensor.html).
