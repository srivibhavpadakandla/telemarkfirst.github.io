# Lesson F1.3: Hub Heading and Reset

The Prime Hub IMU estimates clockwise-positive heading in degrees. Keep the hub fixed in the build and reset heading only while the robot is stopped. When DriveBase uses gyro control, reset through `robot.reset()` instead of `hub.imu.reset_heading()`.

## Worked example

~~~python
robot.stop()
hub.imu.reset_heading(0)
robot.turn(90)
print("Heading:", hub.imu.heading())
~~~

## Simulator practice

Turn right and compare the commanded angle with the reported heading. Reset, then turn left.

## On the robot

Mark north on the practice mat. Test the same turn three times after a steady launch and record heading error.

## Check and adjust

You can state the reset condition and calculate heading error in degrees. If the heading sign is unexpected, check hub orientation and turn direction.
