# Lesson F13.2: Budget the Match

Plan within the 150 second match clock, including launch setup, driving, tool motion, and time reserved for recovery. Convert StopWatch milliseconds to seconds before adding mission times. Use the longest recent trial or another conservative bound instead of the fastest run when deciding what fits.

## Worked example

~~~python
mission_times_s = [32, 41, 28]
launch_s = 5 * len(mission_times_s)
reserve_s = 15
planned_s = sum(mission_times_s) + launch_s + reserve_s
print("Planned seconds:", planned_s)
print("Fits match:", planned_s <= 150)
~~~
