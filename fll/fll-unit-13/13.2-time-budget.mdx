# Lesson F13.2: Budget the Match

The match clock includes handling at home as well as driving and tool motion. We can add those times to see whether a planned set of missions fits within 150 seconds. A reserve for recovery gives the team room when a launch takes longer than expected.

## Worked example

The list contains three mission estimates in seconds. Five seconds is added for each launch, followed by a 15 second reserve; the final Boolean reports whether the total fits.

~~~python
mission_times_s = [32, 41, 28]
launch_s = 5 * len(mission_times_s)
reserve_s = 15
planned_s = sum(mission_times_s) + launch_s + reserve_s
print("Planned seconds:", planned_s)
print("Fits match:", planned_s <= 150)
~~~

## Simulator practice

Use the worked calculation in the editor and replace its mission estimates with measured times. If a stopwatch reports milliseconds, divide by 1000 before adding them to this seconds-based plan.

## On the robot

Physical timing should include program selection, alignment, and attachment handling. The longest recent complete run may be a more useful planning number than the fastest one.

## Check and adjust

The plan should fit inside 150 seconds with its reserve stated. If it does not, shortening a route or removing an objective may be more realistic than using only best-case times.
