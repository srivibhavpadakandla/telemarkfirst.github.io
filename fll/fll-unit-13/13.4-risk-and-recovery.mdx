# Lesson F13.4: Plan a Skip Decision

Decide before the match which mission to skip when time is short or a cue fails. Use a clear condition based on remaining time or a failed result, and follow a tested fallback instead of improvising an unsafe route under the clock.

## Worked example

~~~python
time_left_s = 38
target_ready = False
if time_left_s >= 30 and target_ready:
    print("Run target mission")
else:
    print("Run short return mission")
~~~

## Simulator practice

Try three time-left values and both target states. Confirm the printed choice matches the written plan.

## On the robot

Rehearse the same decision with a stopwatch and the physical hub slots. Record how long switching programs takes.

## Check and adjust

A teammate can make the skip decision from the written rule without reading code. If fallback selection is slow, rehearse hub slots and tools.
