# Lesson F13.1: Compare Mission Value

Compare a mission's points with its measured success rate and time before choosing its place in the match. Multiplying points by success probability gives a simple expected value, though it does not account for interactions between missions. Record how trials produced the success estimate so the team can judge it alongside route time.

## Worked example

~~~python
points = 20
successes = 4
trials = 5
success_rate = successes / trials
expected_points = points * success_rate
print("Expected points:", expected_points)
~~~
