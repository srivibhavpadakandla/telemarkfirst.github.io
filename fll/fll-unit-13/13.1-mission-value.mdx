# Lesson F13.1: Compare Mission Value

Mission points are only part of a match plan. A route that scores often may contribute more than a higher-value route that rarely succeeds. This lesson uses trial results to estimate expected points, then compares that estimate with the time a mission takes.

## Worked example

Four successes in five trials give an estimated success rate of 0.8. Multiplying by the example's 20 points gives a simple expected value; it does not include how this mission affects another route.

~~~python
points = 20
successes = 4
trials = 5
success_rate = successes / trials
expected_points = points * success_rate
print("Expected points:", expected_points)
~~~

## Check and adjust

A useful comparison should show points, trial counts, estimated success rate, and time for both options. Few trials may leave the estimate uncertain, even when the calculated value looks high.
