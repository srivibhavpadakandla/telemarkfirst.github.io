# Lesson F6.4: Represent a Route with a List

A list can hold route steps in order, such as `(300, 90)` for a 300 mm drive followed by a 90 degree right turn. Document the order and units, then use a loop to execute each pair. Keep unlike mission actions outside the list when combining them would make the route harder for teammates to inspect.

## Worked example

~~~python
route = [(300, 90), (50, -90), (150, 0)]
for distance_mm, turn_deg in route:
    robot.straight(distance_mm)
    if turn_deg != 0:
        robot.turn(turn_deg)
~~~
