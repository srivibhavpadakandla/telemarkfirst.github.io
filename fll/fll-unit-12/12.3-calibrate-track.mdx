# Lesson F12.3: Calibrate Axle Track

Calibrate turns after straight travel, using a full 360 degree turn on a clear surface as recommended in the Pybricks reference. Reduce the configured axle track if the robot turns too far, or increase it if the turn is short. Recheck a straight drive afterward, and inspect grip or structure when turn results vary widely.

## Worked example

~~~python
old_track_mm = 112
requested_deg = 360
actual_deg = 375
new_track_mm = old_track_mm * requested_deg / actual_deg
print("Try axle track mm:", new_track_mm)
~~~

## Simulator practice

Run a 360 degree turn, then compare final heading in the automatic profile table. Change axle track in code and rerun.

## On the robot

Measure three full turns from the same mark. Change the track value in small steps and retest straight distance.

## Check and adjust

Your chosen track value improves the turn while retaining the straight calibration. If turn improves but straight worsens, revisit diameter first.

Reference: [Pybricks DriveBase reference](https://docs.pybricks.com/en/stable/robotics.html).
