# Lesson F12.2: Calibrate Wheel Diameter

Measure a long straight drive on the real mat before changing wheel diameter. Reduce the configured diameter when the robot consistently stops short, and increase it when the robot travels too far. Keep axle track fixed during this pass, using `old diameter × actual distance ÷ requested distance` as a starting estimate.

## Worked example

~~~python
old_diameter_mm = 56
requested_mm = 500
actual_mm = 480
new_diameter_mm = old_diameter_mm * actual_mm / requested_mm
print("Try wheel diameter mm:", new_diameter_mm)
~~~

## Simulator practice

Change the wheel diameter in code, rerun a 500 mm drive, and compare the automatic three-profile table after each run.

## On the robot

Measure at least three 500 mm drives. Try the calculated value, then repeat the measurement before accepting it.

## Check and adjust

Your new value reduces consistent straight-line error without relying on one trial. If diameter correction worsens error, check the formula order.
