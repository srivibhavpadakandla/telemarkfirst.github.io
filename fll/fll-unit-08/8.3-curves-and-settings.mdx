# Lesson F8.3: Arcs and Speed Settings

`DriveBase.arc(radius, angle=...)` drives a circular segment with radius in millimeters and angle in degrees. A positive radius makes a right arc. Test a short arc and check the robot's full footprint before increasing the angle. DriveBase settings also use different units: straight speed and acceleration use mm/s and mm/s², while turn rate and acceleration use deg/s and deg/s².

## Worked example

~~~python
robot.settings(160, 320, 90, 180)  # mm/s, mm/s², deg/s, deg/s²
robot.arc(100, angle=30)
robot.arc(-100, angle=30)
~~~
