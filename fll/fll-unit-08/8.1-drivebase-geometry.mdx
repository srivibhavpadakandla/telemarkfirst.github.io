# Lesson F8.1: Wheel Diameter and Axle Track

The DriveBase needs two measurements from the robot: wheel diameter and axle track. Diameter is measured across a wheel; axle track is the distance between the wheel contact points on the floor. We will use these values to compare a straight drive and a turn. The example numbers are starting values, so your own build should be measured before using them on hardware.

## Worked example

Both measurements are in millimeters. A wrong diameter can affect the straight distance, while a wrong axle track can affect the turn angle.

~~~python
WHEEL_DIAMETER_MM = 56
AXLE_TRACK_MM = 112
robot = DriveBase(left_motor, right_motor,
                  wheel_diameter=WHEEL_DIAMETER_MM,
                  axle_track=AXLE_TRACK_MM)
robot.straight(500)
robot.turn(180)
~~~

## Simulator practice

Change the wheel diameter once and compare the reported distance and turn with the first run. This can help separate a geometry setting from a route command.

## On the robot

Measure the wheel and the contact-point spacing on the actual build. A 500 mm straight and a 180 degree turn from marked starts can show which measurement needs another look.

## Check and adjust

Keep the two measurements and their separate errors on the calibration sheet. If straight distance is consistently wrong, check diameter first; a repeatable turn error may point to axle track.
