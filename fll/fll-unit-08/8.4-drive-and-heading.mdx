# Lesson F8.4: Continuous Drive and Heading

`DriveBase.drive(speed, turn_rate)` starts continuous motion and returns immediately, so every route needs a stop condition. Speed is in millimeters per second and turn rate in degrees per second. A heading reading can confirm the direction of travel. If DriveBase gyro control is enabled, reset with `robot.reset()` rather than `hub.imu.reset_heading()`.

## Worked example

~~~python
robot.reset()
watch = StopWatch()
robot.drive(120, 15)
while watch.time() < 1000:
    wait(10)
robot.stop()
print("Heading deg:", hub.imu.heading())
~~~

## Simulator practice

Predict the direction and rough angle after one second. Confirm the stop and printed heading.

## On the robot

Try the continuous drive only in a clear area at reduced speed. Compare heading from the hub with a floor mark.

## Check and adjust

The robot stops after one second and the heading sign matches the turn direction. If continuous motion does not stop, inspect the timer and stop call.
