# Lesson F10.3: Read and Reset Heading

Heading gives the robot a way to check its turn against a reference direction. `PrimeHub.imu.heading()` reports clockwise positive degrees relative to the program start or last reset. This lesson resets the reference through DriveBase, makes a turn, and compares the reported heading with a floor mark.

## Worked example

The drive base is stopped before `robot.reset(angle=0)`. With DriveBase gyro control, a direct IMU reset during driving can raise an error in current Pybricks.

~~~python
robot.stop()
robot.reset(angle=0)
robot.turn(90)
heading_deg = hub.imu.heading()
print("Heading deg:", heading_deg)
~~~

## Simulator practice

Try the worked example's right turn from reset, then change the angle for a left turn. The signs and magnitudes can be compared with the commanded turns.

## On the robot

Let the real robot settle before reading the heading. Three trials against a marked 90 degree floor angle can show whether the turn is consistent.

## Check and adjust

Record the commanded angle, measured heading, and difference for each trial. A repeated difference may point to turn calibration; a varying one may need a closer look at grip or launch position.
