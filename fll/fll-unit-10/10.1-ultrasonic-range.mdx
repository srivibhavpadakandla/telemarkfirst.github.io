# Lesson F10.1: Read Ultrasonic Range

An ultrasonic reading can help the robot judge how far it is from an object. `UltrasonicSensor.distance()` reports millimeters, with 2000 indicating that no valid distance was measured in the current Pybricks API. We will compare readings at two positions and keep an invalid reading separate from a clear path.

## Worked example

The conditional prints either an invalid-range message or a measured distance. A narrow or angled target may give a poor echo, so one reading should not decide a close approach.

~~~python
range_mm = distance_sensor.distance()
if range_mm == 2000:
    print("No valid range")
else:
    print("Range mm:", range_mm)
~~~

## On the robot

A ruler can provide a comparison for the sensor on hardware. Flat and angled targets may produce different results at the same physical gap.

## Check and adjust

The log should distinguish a valid distance from 2000, which means the measurement was unavailable. If invalid readings recur, the target's angle and surface are useful things to check.

Reference: [Pybricks UltrasonicSensor reference](https://docs.pybricks.com/en/stable/pupdevices/ultrasonicsensor.html).
