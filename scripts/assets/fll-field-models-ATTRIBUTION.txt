FLL practice field LEGO CAD props

The four fll-field-*.ldr source assemblies in this directory were arranged for
the Telemark curriculum from official LDraw Parts Library files. Their meshes
are representations of real LEGO parts supplied by the independent LDraw
community; they are not original engineering CAD supplied by the LEGO Group.

Part source: LDraw.org Official Parts Library, 2026-10 complete release
https://library.ldraw.org/updates
https://library.ldraw.org/library/updates/complete.zip
